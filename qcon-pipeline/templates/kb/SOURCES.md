# How claims are marked

Every statement in a brief is one of three kinds.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[S07]`, `[N3]`, `[M4]`, `[V1]` | Captured at the talk: slide photo, typed note, moment marker, our own reflection afterwards |
| `[R2]` | A public source, listed at the end of the brief with its URL and fetch date |
| "Our read:" | Our inference or opinion, not something the speaker said |

A sentence about what the speaker said carries a capture mark. A fact about a speaker, company or
project carries a source mark. A number that comes only from a vendor is reported as the vendor's claim.

`confidence` in each brief's header says how well the brief represents the talk (not whether the
speaker is right). Where captures were thin, the brief says less.

Slide photos are attendees' still photos, which QCon permits; only photos a brief cites are included,
downscaled and credited to the speaker. Official videos: see QCon's terms
(https://qconsf.com/terms-conditions/nov2026); they are linked, never copied.
