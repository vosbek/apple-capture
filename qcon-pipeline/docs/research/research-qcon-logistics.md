## 2. Session videos

- **Who gets them:** in-person tickets include **12 months of on-demand access**. Video-Only Pass ($590) holders get the same library, with **no live stream**. [2026] https://qconsf.com/, https://qconsf.com/faq
- **When:** the FAQ says "The session recordings will be available starting on **December 21st, 2026**". That is about 5 weeks after the event. The Terms (Rev. June 25, 2026, §25) were changed "from 4 wks to ca. 5 wks post-conference". **Some older FAQ text still says "within around two weeks"**, so treat Dec 21 as authoritative. [2026] https://qconsf.com/faq, https://qconsf.com/terms-conditions
- **Through what:** the attendee's QCon website login (My Account) in a browser. Chrome is recommended, and **Safari is not supported**. Captions only come from Chrome's live captions, because the videos carry no subtitles. **Downloading is not permitted** (§25: "meant for personal viewing by Event ticket holders only and downloading of the videos is not permitted"). The InfoQ Mobile app also advertises recorded QCon video playback. [2026] FAQ, terms, App Store listing
- **Coverage is not all sessions.** The FAQ says "a majority of keynote and presentation recordings". The schedule tags some sessions **"No Video Available"**, so you can tell ahead of time which talks you **must** capture yourself. [2026] FAQ. Tags seen on the 2026 schedule and the 2025 schedule https://qconsf.com/schedule/nov2025
- **Public release later (InfoQ):** talks appear free on infoq.com/presentations with a **full transcript**, summary and bio, staggered over many months. The QCon SF 2025 videos were still being published in **Aug–Oct 2026, 9–11 months later**. [prior year] https://www.infoq.com/qcon-san-francisco-2025/presentations/, https://www.infoq.com/presentations/mergequeue/
- **Fast written coverage:** InfoQ editors publish **news write-ups of selected talks during and within about a week of the event**. For SF 2025 there were 12 items between Nov 18 and Nov 27, including "Developing Claude Code at Anthropic at AI Speed". [prior year] https://www.infoq.com/qcon-san-francisco-2025/news

## 3. Slides

- Slides are **optional for speakers**. The FAQ says: "If the speaker agreed to share their slides, they will be made available in the schedule and on the presentation page within days." [2026] https://qconsf.com/faq
- **Per-talk pages exist** at `qconsf.com/presentation/nov2026/{slug}` and keynotes at `qconsf.com/keynote/nov2026/{slug}`.
- **[unverified] Real availability looks thin or login-gated.** Public post-event pages checked for 2025 and 2024 had **no slides link and no video link**: https://qconsf.com/presentation/nov2025/how-build-exchange, https://qconsf.com/presentation/nov2024/persona-lized-problem-solving-clojure, https://qconsf.com/schedule/nov2025. Slides may only show when you are logged in. Plan as though most decks will **not** be available.
- Public InfoQ video pages have a "View Presentation" mode that shows synced slides, but no PDF download was confirmed. [prior year]

## 4. Recording / photography policy (exact text found)

**Terms & Conditions, §24 "Your Event Photos"** ([2026], Rev. June 25, 2026). https://qconsf.com/terms-conditions
> "If you like, you may take your own still-photos/screenshots during the Event and share them on social media."
> "You are not permitted to record or otherwise make your own video of any session."

The same section says posts must not infringe others' privacy, publicity or IP rights, and that violators may be sanctioned or expelled without refund.

**§25 On-Demand Video Access:**
> "These videos are meant for personal viewing by Event ticket holders only and downloading of the videos is not permitted."

**§34 Copyrights:**
> "C4Media holds all trademarks and copyrights for the content of the conferences and Events."

**Code of Conduct** (QCon and InfoQ). https://qconferences.com/code-conduct
> "Harassing or unwanted photography of other attendees is strictly prohibited."
> "Always ask for consent before photographing or filming other participants."
> "The content shared during peer-sharing sessions or in chats should remain private [and] should not be shared outside of the event or program unless mutually agreed to by all participants involved."

**Interpretation:**
- **Photos of slides are explicitly allowed.**
- **Video recording of sessions is explicitly prohibited.**
- **Audio-only recording is not addressed** anywhere found: not in the terms, the CoC or the FAQ. This is a grey area, not a permission. The ban on making "your own video of any session" plus C4Media's copyright claim suggests it is unwelcome. The safe options are to ask the speaker or info@qconsf.com, or to use audio only as a private, ephemeral transcription aid that is never shared raw.
- **Unconference and lunch-table content should not go into a shared repo verbatim.** The CoC's peer-sharing confidentiality line covers it.

## 5. Practical: devices, Wi-Fi, app, badge

- **Wi-Fi:** "Yes, wifi will be available at QCon San Francisco." Credentials are printed on your badge. [2026] FAQ. The Hyatt also has free lobby Wi-Fi (venue page).
- **Power at sessions / laptops:** **[unverified]** Nothing official found. Assume limited outlets in ballrooms and bring a battery pack. Phone and tablet note-taking is the norm, but this is general conference practice, not QCon-sourced.
- **Official app: "InfoQ Mobile"** (iOS, C4Media, v2.1.3 dated Oct 23). Features: the **full schedule by day, track and room**, **starred sessions as a personal agenda**, abstracts, speaker bios, track hosts, **session rating and speaker surveys** ("while the talk is still fresh"), push alerts, floor plans, social events, CoC/FAQ, connecting with attendees, Watch Later or reading list, and recorded video playback. [2026 listing] https://apps.apple.com/us/app/infoq-mobile/id6479538837
  - **Android version: [unverified]**. Only the iOS listing was found.
  - **Talk IDs/URLs in the app: [unverified]**. Not documented. The **website** has stable slugs, and there is an **LLM-readable program file** at https://qconsf.com/llms/nov2026.md that is linked from every presentation page. Use those as the canonical talk key.
- **Ratings:** green / yellow / red / "super green" through the app, or on the website when logged in. Top-rated talks from earlier days get mentioned in later track intros. [2026] FAQ. Prior-year observation: https://blog.thestateofme.com/2026/03/28/qcon-london-2026/
- **Badge:** sponsors can only get your contact details if you let them scan the **QR code on the back of your badge**. Badges can't be shared, and you need photo ID to collect one. Hashtags: #QConSF #QCon #QConCares. [2026] FAQ

## 6. Speaker resources on talk pages

- **Presentation page** (for example https://qconsf.com/presentation/nov2026/revenge-data-scientist-why-reliable-ai-needs-evals-traces-and-metrics) has the title, full abstract, a **"Key Takeaways" list** on some talks, time, room, track and host, topic tags, the speaker name, title and company, a link to `qconsf.com/speakers/{name}`, and other talks in the same track. Some 2025 pages also carry an **"Interview"** Q&A with the speaker; the schedule tags these "Interview Available". [2026], with the interview detail [prior year]
- **Speaker page** (for example https://qconsf.com/speakers/manjurajashekhar) has the bio, company and role, pronouns, and **LinkedIn / X / GitHub links when the speaker provided them**, plus their talks. [2026]
- **Not linked:** related InfoQ articles, podcasts or past talks did **not** appear on either page type. These have to be looked up separately on infoq.com, which has a profile page per speaker (for example `infoq.com/profile/{Name}`). [2026]

## 7. Tips from past attendees and organizers

- **Hallway track and socials are a major source of value.** Plan on breaks, lunch topic tables and receptions as much as talks. https://blog.thestateofme.com/2026/03/28/qcon-london-2026/
- **Choose talks by speaker and topic you trust, then use ratings.** Later-day track intros call out the top-rated talks. (same source)
- **Go outside your lane at least once.** Attendees reported surprise value from tracks they wouldn't normally pick. https://www.infoq.com/news/2024/12/takeaways-qcon-infoq-dev-summits
- **Lean on InfoQ write-ups first.** Videos and transcripts take months to go public. (thestateofme)
- **Note-heavy attendees bring value back to teams.** The 2025 recap quotes a first-timer with about 25 pages of notes, 20 talks and 12 connections, who planned to apply the takeaways with their teams. https://qconsf.com/recap/nov2025
- **Book training early.** Some Thu/Fri sessions are already sold out. [2026] https://qconsf.com/

## What this means for the app

- **No video capture. Photos are fine.** QCon explicitly allows still photos of slides and bans session video (§24). Build around **phone photos with timestamps and the talk ID**. Treat **audio** as a grey-area, private transcription aid: it is not addressed, and it is not to be shared raw or committed to the shared repo.
- **No need to scrape or keep the schedule by hand.** Seed talk metadata from `https://qconsf.com/llms/nov2026.md`, the presentation and speaker pages, and the schedule. Use the `presentation/nov2026/{slug}` URL as the **canonical talk ID**, and match captures to talks by **room + time slot** (50-min slots at fixed times). Leave bookmarking and ratings to the InfoQ Mobile app rather than rebuilding them.
- **Plan for slides not to be available.** Slides are optional, appear "within days" at best, and showed no public evidence on past pages. **Your photos are the slide record.** Add a later "enrich" step that checks the presentation page for slides once logged in.
- **Treat official video as a deferred enrichment.** Recordings land around **Dec 21, 2026** and are streaming-only with no download. Public InfoQ transcripts come months later. The app should **sync day-of notes now** and **re-link the official recording or transcript later**. Use the "No Video Available" tags to flag the talks where your own capture is the only record.
- **Speaker research must go beyond qconsf.com.** Talk pages give the bio, company and sometimes LinkedIn/X/GitHub. InfoQ articles, podcasts and past talks are **not linked**, so Claude should search infoq.com profiles, InfoQ news (write-ups appear within about a week) and the speaker's GitHub.
- **Keep confidential content out of the shared repo.** Unconference and lunch-table discussions fall under the CoC's peer-sharing confidentiality line. Tag them `private` and exclude them from internal publishing by default.
